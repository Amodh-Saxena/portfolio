Chapter 02 · About

Bridging the gap between a notebook demo and a production system.

I spent the early years of my degree learning that the gap between a notebook demo and a production system is where most student projects die. So I started shipping things that actually run — on my own hardware, with zero paid APIs, solving problems that exist outside classrooms.

My work lives at the intersection of agentic AI, geospatial systems, and fintech ML. I build end-to-end: from data pipelines and model architecture through to API layers and frontend interfaces. Everything I ship is constrained by real hardware — 16GB RAM, RTX 4050 6GB VRAM — which forces better engineering decisions.

Currently targeting AI/ML/DS internships and MSc programmes in Germany. I lead committees at Aarush and manage projects at Cherry+ Network alongside the coursework.
